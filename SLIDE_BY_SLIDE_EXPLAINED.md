## Main sequence — 18 minutes

### main-01 — The case question (45 seconds)

Frame the question: which channels and content formats support acquisition, recorded revenue, and engagement? Answer first: referral is the strongest new-logo source and marketing-sourced deals convert lowest. Fix conversion and value capture, then test before scaling.

### main-02 — Marketing strategy (60 seconds)

Protect referral acquisition, fix marketing-sourced conversion before adding volume, record contract value on every win, and use controlled creative and account-coverage tests before expanding spend. Attribution can generate hypotheses; only a controlled comparison can establish incremental lift.

### main-03 — Evidence scope (60 seconds)

CRM history begins in 2018. Email, web and 6sense logs cover 2024, and ad metrics span January 2023 to October 2024. Historic opportunity records therefore have a bounded marketing observation window. Separate what the CRM records from what the activity logs can link.

### main-04 — Business motion (60 seconds)

A deal counts as acquisition when its account had no earlier won deal. This classifies every year, including 2023–24, where the CRM type field is blank. Existing customers account for 71.6% of wins.

### main-05 — Channel effectiveness (90 seconds)

The current canonical population has 3,245 opportunities, $24.9M in recorded pipeline, and $5.4M in recorded won amount. Referral leads recorded new-logo won amount across 2018–2024. Recorded amount omits $3.4M of monthly recurring revenue on zero-amount wins, so read amount beside MRR and win-rate intervals.

### main-06 — Recent conversion by source (60 seconds)

In 2023–24 new-logo deals, marketing-sourced opportunities win 10.2% of resolved deals, the lowest of five source groups, against 26.8% for referral. Marketing supplied 382 of 1,192 new-logo opportunities. Some 2024 deals are still open.

### main-07 — Conversion drivers (90 seconds)

The 2022 H1 to 2024 H1 conversion-rate change is decomposed into mix shifts and within-group conversion changes. Both components are descriptive: neither one alone identifies a cause.

### main-08 — Advertising content (90 seconds)

Only five ads have complete creative metadata. Treat Blog / Problem / FOMO as a bundle hypothesis, not evidence that any one creative attribute wins. Compare ads within platform and prioritize metadata completion before making broader creative claims.

### main-09 — Email and website engagement (75 seconds)

Email results describe conditional engagement in the supplied event logs; no delivered-message denominator supports a standard send-based rate. A web contact-page activity flag records observed behavior but does not prove completed acquisition.

### main-10 — Source and touch overlap (60 seconds)

CRM source credit and touch-linked populations overlap. The four exclusive groups make that overlap explicit, so differently defined attribution totals are not added together.

### main-11 — Attribution and journeys (75 seconds)

Channel rankings change by attribution model and lookback window. The journey views describe linked paths before opportunity creation, not causal contribution. 684 of the 1,191 opportunities created in 2024 (57%) link to a prior touch; earlier years cannot link because the touch logs cover 2024 only. About 79% of usable touches are 6sense ad impressions aimed at in-market accounts, so linked credit overstates influence.

### main-12 — Target audience (60 seconds)

After customer, pipeline, target, and profile exclusions, 2,034 eligible ICP domains remain for a coverage test. Observed reach differences are not randomized results; use this eligible population to design the experiment.

### main-13 — Channel and content actions (75 seconds)

Fix marketing-sourced conversion, protect referral, record contract value, and test creative and account coverage under control.

### main-14 — Controlled experiment (75 seconds)

Randomize eligible domains 50/50 and measure new CRM opportunity creation after 90 days before evaluating broader commercial outcomes. At a 5% baseline and a +2 percentage-point lift, the design requires 4,426 domains; only 2,034 are available, so that target is infeasible at the current population size. Use the delivered power scenarios to choose a detectable effect or a longer accumulation plan.

### main-15 — Budget implications (45 seconds)

Treat historical tracked spend as context. Size the planning budget from sample feasibility and cost per enrolled treatment account; do not present it as a revenue forecast.

### main-16 — CMO decisions (60 seconds)

Fix marketing-sourced conversion and value capture now, protect referral acquisition, and approve controlled creative and account-coverage tests. Expand only when the controlled results support it.
